# Scheetz2006 source terms and rights review

The measurements come from the authors' [GSE5680 submission to
GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE5680). The
[paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1636701/) identifies that
accession as its data deposit. Preparation uses the processed series matrix, not
Iowa's RDS, local text file, code, or annotations. Iowa's dataset serves as a
comparison reference for the probe selection and rounded values.

The [GEO disclaimer](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/disclaimer.html)
states that NCBI places no restrictions on use or distribution of GEO data. It
also warns that submitters may assert intellectual property rights and that NCBI
cannot grant unrestricted permission on their behalf. Its copyright section
permits downloading and reproducing files unless otherwise stated, subject to
any applicable rights asserted by copyright holders.

The reviewed series record, its sample metadata in the downloaded matrix, and
the sample processing record for
[GSM130491](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM130491) did
not state an additional data-specific restriction. The paper's copyright notice
covers the article; this preparation does not redistribute its prose, figures,
or supplementary documents.

The assessment treats GEO's stated use and distribution policy as the source
terms for redistributing this numeric extract. Retain source identification, the
study citation, and the preparation details. Record the policy URL and its
qualifications, without inferring CC0, CC BY, or another SPDX license. The
repository's MIT license covers the new scripts and documentation only.

The source-policy review supplies the redistribution basis for the numeric
extract. Publication and download verification are recorded separately in
`zenodo-record.json`; they do not establish an independent registry release
attestation.
