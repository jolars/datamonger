# Koussounadis2014 source terms and rights review

Preparation uses the authors' [GSE49577 submission to
GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE49577): the
processed expression matrix and supplementary phenodata table. It also uses
the `ID` and `ILMN_Gene` identifiers from Illumina's [GPL10558
platform](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GPL10558).
The [paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/bjc.2014.258) identifies GSE49577 as its
data deposit. Iowa's export is a comparison reference only. No Iowa file or
code is redistributed or required by the preparation.

The [GEO disclaimer](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/disclaimer.html)
states that NCBI places no restrictions on use or distribution of submitted
data. It also warns that submitters may claim intellectual property rights
and that NCBI cannot grant unrestricted permission on their behalf. Its
copyright section permits downloading and reproducing files unless otherwise
stated, subject to rights asserted by copyright holders.

The reviewed GSE49577 metadata, its downloaded sample metadata and phenodata,
the [GSM1202097 processing
record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM1202097), and
GPL10558's platform metadata state no additional data-specific restriction.
GPL10558 describes its platform distribution as commercial; that describes
the microarray product and supplies no separate dataset license. The extract
retains internal feature identifiers while omitting probe sequences,
descriptions, ontology annotations, and other platform fields.

The paper's rights notice licenses the article under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 after
twelve months. This preparation does not redistribute article prose,
figures, or publisher supplementary documents. The review does not assume
that the article license applies to the separate GEO deposit or replace
GEO's qualified source terms with a named Creative Commons license.

stated use and distribution policy as the redistribution basis for the
numeric extract and retained source identifiers. Retain source identification,
