# Glioma source rights review

The preparation input is the publisher's Supplementary Table S2 for
[Godlewski et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-38243-1).
The [full-text article at PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10329700/)
reproduces the article's CC BY 4.0 rights notice and identifies supplementary
data as part of the work in its data-availability statement.

The article's rights notice covers the article's material unless a separate
third-party credit indicates otherwise. The reviewed S2 workbook contains
one complete measurement table, with no separate rights notice or third-party
credit. This automated review applies the article's
[CC BY 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) to that
supplementary table and its lossless CSV representation. This assessment is
based on the article and supplement, not merely public download access.

Redistribution must retain attribution to the original authors and source,
link CC BY 4.0, and identify the XLSX-to-CSV conversion and compression.
`DATASET.md`, uploaded as `README.md`, supplies that information. No scientific
transformation, sample exclusion, or label change is made by Datamonger.
The original authors' normalization, filtering, and imputation remain part
of the source data and are documented separately.

The SLOPE package identifies the source workbook but supplies neither the
input values nor preparation code for this conversion. Its binary label
mapping and exclusion of meningioma samples are not used. Supplementary
Table S1 contains derived pathway-analysis results and is not redistributed.
